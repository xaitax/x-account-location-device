Community Cloud Cache ON BY DEFAULT

Privacy Notice: This is on unless you turn it off, so that flags still work when X rate-limits your lookups. While it is on, public profile facts are shared with a community server, keyed by username: the country and device X reports, the account's affiliation badge, its creation date, its numeric account ID, and how many times the handle has changed. All of it is already public on the account's own profile. Nothing about you is sent: no identity, no browsing history, no list of what you looked at. Switching it off below stops both sharing and receiving; direct lookups keep working.
