What's New in X-Posed v3.4

Block whole organisations, and no more posts slipping through

Thank you. X-Posed has come a long way, and the community cache just passed 3.2 million accounts. The feedback has been overwhelming: bug reports, feature ideas, people spreading the word. A lot of it is already in this release, and there's plenty more coming. Thanks as well to everyone chipping in to keep the growing server costs under control. It genuinely makes a difference.

Buy me a coffee
Block by affiliation X shows the parent company on affiliated accounts, so blocking one organisation hides all of its staff accounts at once. Type the name as text, and partial names work. It's in the new Affiliations tab, both here and in the sidebar.
Follower lists are flagged too Accounts matching your filters now get an amber accent in Followers, Verified followers and Following. They're only ever flagged there, never hidden, so the lists stay complete and the counts still add up.
Hovering saves more Opening an account's info card now also records its affiliation, age and ID. If an account ever slips past the affiliation filter, hover it once.
Quoted posts are filtered too A blocked country, region, or tag now collapses the quote card as well, with a "click to show" placeholder. The post doing the quoting stays put.
Country blocking fixed Some countries quietly never matched, because X spells them differently than the picker did. Dozens of territories and a couple of regions were missing entirely. All added and fixed, so your blocks now apply everywhere they should.
No more posts vanishing for no reason With VPN/proxy hiding on, a post that quoted a proxied account disappeared entirely. VPN hiding now only applies to a post's own author.
